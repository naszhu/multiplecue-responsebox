PRACTICE 1

In the following, you will see a black point in the center of the screen. Please always fixate this point when you start a trial. 

Upon pressing *space*, a colored circle with a digit from 1 to 4 will appear in the center of the screen.

__________

Your task is report the color of the circle by pressing one of the four keys on the self-made-response-box.

First, rest both hands naturally on the response box: lightly spread your left fingers over the five keys on the left, and do the same with your right fingers over the five keys on the right.

The colors for your left middle finger, left index finger, right middle finger, and right index finger are {C0}, {C1}, {C2}, and {C3}. That is, the left-most is {C0}, the second from the left is {C1}, the right-most is {C2}, and the second from the right is {C3}. The color-key mapping is also indicated by the colors shown on the keys.

The order of the colors relative to the keys is shown below the circle.

__________

You will earn points by reporting the color of the circle correctly while also responding as quickly as possible.

The digit 1 allows you to earn 1 point, and so on until a maximum reward of 4 points per trial. 

Your collected points will be translated into a monetary reward that is added to your compensation payment. Each point is worth 0.05 DKK. After each trial, you will see how many points you have earned in that trial relative to what you could have earned, along with the amount of money in DKK that you have obtained. 
You will also see the speed of your responses. Please, try to be as fast as possible while still earning as many points as possible.
__________

Remember to fixate the black point in the center at the start of each trial.  

Press *space* to begin.
